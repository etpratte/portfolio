Quadruped Robot

2026

Designed and built a custom quadruped robot, developing the mechanical and electronic systems including the chassis and leg assemblies. All structural parts are 3D printed for rapid prototyping and iteration. The robot features 12 DoF, with three 10.2kg-cm servo motors per leg enabling multi-axis movement and is powered by an 11.1V LiPo battery. Leg motion is driven by inverse kinematics and a custom gait algorithm to provide smooth, stable walking across varying speeds and directions. An accelerometer enables self-balancing by continuously adjusting leg positioning in response to orientation changes, while ESP-NOW provides wireless control for real-time maneuvering.

Foldable Quadcopter Drone Airframe

2026

Designed and modeled a personal foldable quadcopter in SolidWorks, developing the airframe and mechanical components including the central body, folding arms, landing legs, propeller blades, and camera mounts, designed to be made from carbon fiber. The design incorporates a folding mechanism that allows the drone to collapse into a more compact configuration for easier storage and transport, and is built to accommodate brushless motors as a camera drone.
